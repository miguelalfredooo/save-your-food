# Pantry

Shelf-stable reference. No dates, no expiry tracking — these don't spoil on a
timescale where a nudge helps.

- plain line     = you have it
- `(low)`        = last of it, buy soon
- `(out)`        = gone
- `?`            = unconfirmed; inferred, not stated
- `(fridge)`     = lives in the fridge but keeps for months

`(low)` and `(out)` become items on the grocery list. Everything else is here so
recipe matching knows what you already own.

Group into sections that match how your kitchen is actually arranged, not a
taxonomy. Some starting ones:
